How To Setup NFC Cards In Your Restaurant

Standard NTAG213 / NTAG215 cards cost only ₹15 to ₹25 each on Amazon and last for years without batteries.

1

Get Blank NFC Cards

Order standard NTAG213 or NTAG215 PVC cards or stickers from Amazon (approx ₹20/unit).

2

Download Free App

Install the free "NFC Tools" app on Android (Google Play) or iPhone (App Store).

3

Write Table URL

Select "Write URL" in the app and paste your table link e.g. /menu.html?table=1. Tap the card!

4

Place on Table

Place the acrylic stand on Table 1. Whenever a customer taps, Table 1's menu opens instantly!

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About TipTap NFC

What if a customer's phone does not have NFC? ▼

All TipTap acrylic table cards are dual-designed: they have the smart NFC chip inside, plus a printed backup QR code at the corner. Over 92% of modern phones (iPhone 7+, Samsung, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Vivo, Oppo) have NFC active by default. For older devices, guests can simply scan the backup QR code.

Does the guest or waiter need to install any app? ▼

Zero app installation required! Tapping the NFC card automatically opens the restaurant's menu in Safari (on iOS) or Google Chrome (on Android) in under half a second.

Can customers tamper with prices or quantities? ▼

Never. When an order is placed, only item IDs and quantities are sent to the TipTap server. Prices, taxes (5% GST), and totals are strictly computed on the backend server from your official menu database.
